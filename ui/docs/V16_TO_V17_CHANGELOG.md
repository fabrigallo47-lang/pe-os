# V16 to V17

## Preserved from V16

- dark command-center visual system;
- question-first Deal Overview;
- workstream execution view;
- Evidence Aperture and exact-source inspection;
- two-step professional Change Review;
- animated propagation sequence;
- Artifacts and Journal;
- Associate/Partner projection;
- local/offline opening and deterministic demo scenes.

## Added in V17

- Fund Command and Morning Delta;
- universal shell across Fund and Deal scales;
- explicit Command, Evidence, Economics, Work, Relationships and Time lenses;
- rooms for Foundations, Unknowns and Shadow IC;
- Scenario Lab;
- formal Decision Room;
- controlled Execution Room;
- Causal Replay across Known, Believed, Approved and Open;
- backend projection adapter;
- API integration layer and mock server;
- explicit ownership contracts;
- projection and screen schemas;
- structural self-test;
- one continuous product demo and screenshot set.

## Removed from the frontend's responsibility

V16 contained prewritten propagation and local rule logic. V17 keeps those fixtures only for offline demonstration. In connected mode, consequences, financial values, policy results, human stops and settlement come from the backend.

## Important disclosure

Several V16 demo economics, including the concentration-driven supported ceiling, are preserved as synthetic product-experience fixtures because they demonstrate the intended interaction. They must not be represented as sourced Keystone Real outputs unless the relevant policy and model objects are valid in the connected backend.
