# Project Keystone - Financial Due Diligence and Quality of Earnings Report

## Executive findings

Alderstone generated FY2025 reported revenue of **$74.0m** and reported EBITDA of **$10.2m**. Based on the procedures performed and the adjustments below, normalized FY2025 EBITDA is **$11.9m**.

## Scope and basis of presentation

The work focused on historical revenue, gross profit, EBITDA adjustments, working capital, debt and debt-like items, and selected customer and project data. The procedures do not constitute an audit, examination or valuation opinion.

## Normalized EBITDA schedule

| --- | --- | --- |

| Founder / executive compensation | 0.35 | QoE accepted at market replacement cost |

| Revenue cut-off | -0.1 | QoE correction |
| Bonus accrual normalization | 0.2 | Approved plan support |
| Related-party rent normalization | 0.1 | Not recognized in covenant definition |

## Adjustment conclusions

- Founder and executive compensation was accepted at market replacement cost.
- Transaction-readiness and professional-fee adjustments exclude recurring audit and compliance support.
- Integration and systems costs were accepted only for identifiable non-recurring items.
- Pricing and utilization initiatives were not accepted as historical earnings adjustments.
- Revenue cut-off reduced EBITDA by $0.1m.
- Bonus accrual normalization was supported by the approved plan and subsequent reversal.
- Related-party rent was normalized to market terms.

## Working-capital findings

The seller proposed a **$7.7m** target. Normalized operating working capital is **$8.4m**. The difference reflects seasonal payroll accruals, aged unbilled WIP, acquired-branch billing delays, deferred-revenue normalization and month-end timing.

## Other observations

- Customer master is maintained at billing-account level; parent-company aggregation is a separate analytical exercise.
- Acquired offices use different project, time-entry and billing processes.
- Unbilled WIP increased faster than revenue and aging controls are inconsistent.
- Capitalized systems expenditure and EBITDA add-backs must not duplicate the same cost.
