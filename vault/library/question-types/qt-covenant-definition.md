# What EBITDA basis and add-back limits will lenders use for covenant calculation?

## What resolves it
`attested` credit agreement terms beat the seller's underwritten EBITDA. Framework:
- Covenant EBITDA ≠ QoE EBITDA: lenders apply their own adjustment caps (typically 15-25%)
- The starting cushion (covenant headroom at close) determines how much operational slack exists
- Maintenance vs. incurrence covenants: maintenance tested quarterly; incurrence only on actions
Cross-deal standard: document the lender's EBITDA definition and map it vs. management basis.
