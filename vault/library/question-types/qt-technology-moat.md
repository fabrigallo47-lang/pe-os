# Is the technology defensible, and can the team scale it to plan volumes?

## What resolves it
`attested` IP register, third-party technical review, or certified-supplier qualification evidence beats company white papers.
Look for: software margins assumed 60-80%+ on first principles — verify against comparable SaaS (not hardware-embedded software); hardware-to-software transition risk ("we're becoming a software company"); production scale-up history (units delivered vs. units contracted); certification lead times that gate revenue.
Cross-deal standard: technology moat requires at least one of: patent protection in core markets, regulatory qualification that takes >18 months to replicate, or proprietary dataset that is not rebuildable from public sources.
Space/defence: qualification cascade matters — a new variant may require full re-qualification even if 90% identical to a cleared design.
Red flag: margin profile assumes 70%+ software mix from a company that has delivered <20 software units; serial-production ramp assumed within 12 months of first delivery.
