# PANTA UX Review Checklist

Use this after any meaningful UI/product change.

## Five-second test
- What is the most important truth on screen?
- What is the investor supposed to do next?
- Is the current room's job obvious without explanation?

## Auditability
- Can important statements/numbers be inspected?
- Does the interface say why we believe it, what is missing, why it matters, and what changed?
- Are related paths meaningful rather than engagement bait?

## Authority
- Is machine synthesis clearly different from human Position?
- Does a governed institutional change require the right human action?
- Is hypothetical state visibly separate from live state?

## Visual maturity
- Does the screen feel purpose-built rather than generated?
- Are there too many cards/labels/status chips?
- Is whitespace deliberate?
- Is text wrapping clean?
- Does the room have a distinct geometry appropriate to its task?

## Copy
Delete any sentence that merely narrates the interface.
Delete ontology/system language a normal investor would not use.

## Cross-product consistency
- Global shell matches.
- Shared actions behave the same way.
- Object Lens/provenance language is consistent.
- Room-specific composition remains distinct.
