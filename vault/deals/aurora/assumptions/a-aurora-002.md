# ARR compounds above 25% per year for the 5-year hold

## Why the deal rests on this
The entire return is carried by growth compounding at a flat multiple: entry 6.5x ARR, exit 6.5x ARR, so the 3x comes from ARR alone. Below ~25% CAGR the 3x degrades toward 1.8x and the deal fails the fund's floor. The near-term anchor is the FY26 plan: management asserts 34% ([[c-aurora-001]]) and the QoE adviser derives 31% on normalized ARR ([[c-aurora-006]]), but the deal team's own model derives only 28% ([[c-aurora-002]]) — an unresolved contradiction that [[q-aurora-growth-real]] exists to close. Note the arithmetic dependency: at NRR of 115% ([[a-aurora-001]]), growth above 25% requires the new-bookings contribution in [[a-aurora-003]].
