# How concentrated is the revenue base after grouping billing accounts by ultimate parent?

## What resolves it
`attested` parent-company registry or group-ownership data beats the CIM billing account list.
Look for cases where billing-account concentration (7-8%) hides ultimate-parent concentration (15-20%+).
The dangerous pattern: a single corporate family buys separately but could terminate together.
Cross-deal standard: concentration >15% at ultimate-parent level = material risk; >25% = red flag.
