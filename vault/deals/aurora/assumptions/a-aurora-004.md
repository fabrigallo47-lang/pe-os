# The exit multiple holds at or above the entry multiple of 6.5x ARR

## Why the deal rests on this
"3x with no multiple expansion" quietly assumes no multiple *contraction* either: the return math prices exit at the entry multiple of 6.5x ARR. Every point of contraction comes straight out of the MOIC — at 25% ARR CAGR, an exit near 4.3x turns the 3x into a 2x and the deal fails the fund's floor even if growth and retention both hold. No claim in the vault currently bears on this (basis is empty), and until this proposal no question tested it — it was the unexamined leg of the thesis. Tested by [[q-aurora-exit-multiple]].
