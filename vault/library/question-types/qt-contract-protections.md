# Which agreements have minimum-volume, take-or-pay, or termination-fee protections?

## What resolves it
`attested` contract extracts beat management summaries. Look for:
- Change-of-control clauses (may trigger consent or termination rights)
- Auto-renewal vs opt-in renewal mechanics
- Minimum spend or volume commitments
- Notice periods and termination cure windows
Cross-deal standard: >50% of revenue under contracts with minimum protections = acceptable baseline.
