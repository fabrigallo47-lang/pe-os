# PANTA V20 Venture Golden Path

## Objective

Demonstrate that the same PANTA kernel handles a sparse-evidence pre-revenue deep-tech venture case without turning conversation or AI output into ungoverned truth.

## Sequence

1. Open Project Tethys in Mock Connected mode.
2. In Deal Command, show the venture archetype and switch from Venture Core to Deep-Tech Validation. Confirm the facts hash is unchanged while the question order moves.
3. Open Sources and show the customer Interaction and Utterance. Explain the observed-speech-act versus asserted-content boundary.
4. Open Compiler Review. Compare the asserted 800 m range, controlled 720 m result and approximately 98 m pilot-implied radius.
5. Open the Derivation object and inspect formula, inputs, assumptions and method hash.
6. Show that AI explanations remain hypotheses and are not propagation-eligible before review.
7. Open Foundations and compare validation envelopes by target, environment and configuration.
8. Open Unknowns and prepare a governed mission. Show that human contact and a physical test cannot auto-run.
9. Open Scenario Lab and compare base, six-month-delay and milestone-tranche paths, cap table and follow-on reserve.
10. Admit the coverage-restatement treatment. In Change Impact, show the Human Stop and scoped authority requirement.
11. Attempt settlement without authority and observe server refusal.
12. Attest a course, obtain the course-specific package, receive simulated acknowledgement and settle.
13. Run the maintenance-gap scenario in a new session. Show the blocked component and explicit bounded partial settlement.
14. Open Registry and Causal Replay to reconstruct what was known and decided.

## Narrative close

PANTA does not win by producing one impressive answer. It wins by preserving who said what, what was known, which reasoning was deterministic or hypothetical, what changed, what remained blocked, who authorised the risk and how the case can be replayed later.
