# PANTA Product Doctrine

## One-sentence product
PANTA makes the institutional investment case a shared, live, manipulable object.

## Purpose
Reduce time to warranted institutional clarity, improve judgment, and multiply what each investor can accomplish.

## Product mantra
**Top-down clarity. Bottomless depth.**

**PANTA is a living operating object.**

## Experience model
The centre shows the situation. The interface helps the investor steer it.

PANTA is not:
- a dashboard collection;
- a chatbot with finance context;
- an agent console;
- a document generator;
- a task manager;
- an ontology editor exposed to investors.

PANTA should continuously maintain the case while judgment remains human.

## Proactivity
When material arrives or mapped state changes, PANTA should do mechanical work automatically:
- ingest/extract;
- reconcile;
- detect stale assumptions and contradictions;
- prepare Findings;
- prepare impact traces;
- prepare artifact diffs and case-backed updates.

The investor should experience the case as having moved intelligently while they were away.

Do not represent proactivity with status theatre. Surface meaningful results where they matter.

## Authority
PANTA may synthesize readings and propose work.
PANTA never fabricates a HumanPosition.
Human/institutional authority remains required for governed judgment, external action, spend, publication, and explicit adoption where required by policy.

## Auditability
Everything important should be touchable.

Investor language:
- Why we believe this
- Still missing
- Why it matters
- What changed
- Where else this appears

Trace is the deep audit surface; auditability is present everywhere.

## Outputs
IC Memo, Model, and Decision Pack are live writable projections of the case.
They can be created/synchronized from case state, directly edited, audited, simulated, and traversed back into the case.

PANTA vs a static memo/model should feel like Excel vs a printed screenshot of Excel.

## Connected exploration
A user should naturally travel through meaningful case relationships:

sentence → reading → source → assumption → model → simulation → condition → decision

Do not build an engagement feed. Show only genuinely useful connected context.

## Frontend language
The engine may be sophisticated. The investor should not need to know it.

Never require the investor to decode ontology, philosophy, graph vocabulary, or system implementation terms.
