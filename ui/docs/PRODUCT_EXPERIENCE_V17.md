# Product Experience V17

## Product premise

PANTA is not a collection of dashboards. It is one persistent investment state viewed at different scales, through different lenses and at different moments in the decision process.

The user should always know:

- where they are;
- what changed;
- what the institution currently believes;
- what the case rests on;
- what remains unknown;
- what consequence follows;
- who must act next;
- what is historical, Current, Working or Approved.

## Universal shell

V17 uses one shell across the product:

- the context rail establishes scale, role and navigation;
- the workspace shows the active state and task;
- the action rail shows the selected situation, consequence or next action;
- the causal time rail preserves historical orientation.

Moving from Fund Command to Deal World is a zoom into the same system, not a switch to another product.

## Product flow

The primary flow is:

`Fund Command → Deal World → Object Aperture → Professional Review → Change Impact → Action Frontier → Decision Room → Execution Room → Settled Deal World → Causal Replay`

Not every event requires every room. A non-material treatment can settle after professional review. A material exception with authority consequences enters Decision Room. An external action crosses Execution Room only after authority and pre-flight checks.

## The core surfaces

### Fund Command

Fund Command allocates attention across live investment situations. It shows the Morning Delta, the reasons each object requires attention and the action expected from the current role. Role projection changes emphasis, not facts.

### Deal World

Deal World organizes one investment case around its underwriting-question spine. It shows Current institutional views, economic relevance, strength, owner, open work and the relationship between Working, Current and Approved branches.

### Object Aperture

Object Aperture is the two-gesture drill-down. It opens on the selected object without removing the user from the Deal World. Its four tabs are:

- Basis: exact evidence, source passage, definition, period and perimeter;
- Dependents: what relies on this object and what coverage remains;
- Action: next work, owner and authority path;
- History: state changes, comments, decisions and source versions.

### Change Review

Change Review keeps stochastic interpretation outside the deterministic runtime. It shows the source and the proposed treatment side by side. The professional admits, edits or rejects the treatment before the event enters the Transition Engine.

### Change Impact

Change Impact reveals the ordered transition returned by Anto's engine. Each affected object has an explicit disposition such as Recomputes, Survives, Falls, Rule Switch, Human or Blocked. The browser does not infer these labels.

### Action Frontier

Action Frontier translates a valid Candidate into coordinated work. It presents model, memo, offer, tracker and communication change sets derived from the same transition, together with coverage limits and unresolved objects.

### Decision Room

Decision Room contains one formal authority request. It presents evidence for and against, courses of action, economics, conditions, policy boundary, holder and deadline. Attestation creates an authority record; it is not merely a chat response.

### Execution Room

Execution Room is the externality boundary. It shows the exact action, recipient, attachments, authority record and pre-flight checks. In the V17 demo, all external actions are simulated.

### Causal Replay

Replay reconstructs the case at a historical point across four dimensions:

- Known: information available to the institution;
- Believed: Case Positions held at the time;
- Approved: formal institutional decisions;
- Open: unresolved questions, conditions and work.

Replay does not rewrite history. It explains how the case developed and preserves the path from evidence to action and outcome.

## Rooms

The Deal World contains four high-value rooms that were previously dispersed across the prototype:

- What the deal rests on: minimal support sets and weak floors;
- Everything we still do not know: unknowns ordered by decision value;
- Shadow IC: the strongest case for and against the investment;
- Registry: append-only source, system, human, decision and execution history.

Scenario Lab, Decision Room and Execution Room are special rooms because they create a governed branch, formal authority or external effect. They are entered only when required and return the user to the same Deal World.

## Motion grammar

Motion communicates state change rather than decoration:

- Reveal: a source or object becomes inspectable;
- Bind: evidence is connected to a Case Position or model object;
- Ripple: consequences propagate in engine order;
- Branch: a scenario or Working treatment separates from Current;
- Route: an action is sent to a person or authority;
- Attest: a formal decision is recorded;
- Settle: the accepted consequence becomes part of Current while Approved remains historically intact unless the relevant authority changes it.

## Visual semantics

- cyan: Current and live state;
- violet: Working or scenario branch;
- amber: stale, divergent or threshold condition;
- red: blocked, failed or incoherent;
- gold: authority and attestation;
- muted slate: context and historical state.

The visual system preserves V16's dark command-center language, readable density, centered content, minimum font sizes and high contrast.
