## Case logic

### Standalone base

- Uses approximately 7% organic growth.
- Assumes gradual margin expansion from 15.4% entry firm EBITDA margin.
- Excludes unidentified acquisitions.
- Uses a 9.0x exit multiple.
- Values exit on exact LTM March 31, 2031 EBITDA.

### Standalone downside

- Assumes lower growth, lower margin, higher working-capital pressure and a lower exit multiple.
- Preserves solvency under a customer or integration downside without relying on unidentified acquisitions.

### Standalone upside

- Assumes stronger organic growth, better utilization, stronger cash conversion and a higher exit multiple than the base case.

### Acquisition base

- Includes Sentinel and a second fully funded acquisition.
- Sentinel closes September 30, 2026.
- The second acquisition is funded with $4.0m of DDTL, $5.0m of sponsor equity and $3.5m of cash.
- No unidentified revenue synergies are included.

## Key assumptions carrying the return

- Organic revenue growth.
- Gradual margin improvement through pricing, utilization and project discipline.
- Working-capital management.
- Scheduled debt amortization and cash generation.
- In the acquisition case, execution of identified add-ons at the modeled purchase prices and funding mix.
- No reliance on multiple expansion in the standalone base case.
