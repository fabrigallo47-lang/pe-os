# What drives the gap between gross margin on different revenue streams, and can plan margins be sustained?

## What resolves it
`attested` segment P&L by product line beats blended company-level margins.
Look for: hardware vs. software vs. services margin mix that shifts over time in the plan (the "software uplift" story); pricing initiatives baked into plan EBITDA that are not yet contracted; labour/input cost inflation not reflected in contract pricing; one-time cost savings (headcount, facilities) counted as recurring EBITDA improvement.
Cross-deal standard: plan margins credible only if the last 24 months of actuals show the trajectory is already in motion, not projected to begin at close.
Space/tech mix: hardware margin 15-25%, software license 65-80%, services 30-45% — verify the revenue mix assumption implicit in the EBITDA bridge, not just the blended margin.
Red flag: EBITDA improvement plan that relies on >3 simultaneous margin initiatives (pricing, volume, mix, headcount, procurement) — each adds execution risk multiplicatively, not additively.
