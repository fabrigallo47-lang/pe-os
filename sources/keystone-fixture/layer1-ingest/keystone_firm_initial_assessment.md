# Project Keystone - Initial Assessment

## Recommendation

Continue confirmatory diligence and maintain exclusivity at no more than **$108.0m** of enterprise value. The opportunity is credible, but the Firm should underwrite the business on its own preliminary view of sustainable EBITDA and should not rely on seller-defined add-backs.

## Initial thesis

1. Scaled platform in outsourced technical compliance with long-standing customer relationships.
2. Historical growth and improving margins, but seller EBITDA must be normalized.
3. Organic growth through pricing, customer expansion and new logos.
4. Moderate, gradual margin improvement from utilization and project discipline.
5. Fragmented add-on landscape, excluded from the standalone base case.
6. Return construction should be based on operations and deleveraging, not multiple expansion.

## Preliminary merits

- Alderstone operates in regulated, safety-critical service categories.
- Revenue is diversified by service line and end market.
- Management reports 38% scheduled/programmatic revenue and a further 34% repeat-project revenue.
- Capex is low at approximately 1%-2% of revenue.
- Seller rollover is meaningful at $12.0m.
- Entry debt is moderate relative to firm-underwritten EBITDA.

## Preliminary concern block

### Customer concentration

The seller presents the largest billing account as **7.6%** of revenue. First-pass review of the raw customer master indicates that several billing accounts share the same parent-company relationship. The Firm should complete parent-level aggregation before IC.

### Integration and execution

The Company has completed four acquisitions since 2020, but acquired branches still use partially different systems and processes. The remaining integration program could affect billing, WIP, DSO and customer service if poorly executed.

### EBITDA quality

The seller-adjusted EBITDA figure includes run-rate pricing and utilization initiatives. The Firm should underwrite only implemented or supportable adjustments.

### Working capital

The Company is asset-light but not working-capital-light. Unbilled WIP, AR aging and branch-level billing controls require detailed review.

### Management and reporting

Management is credible, but the finance, reporting and integration functions need to be assessed for sponsor-backed ownership.

## What must be true

1. The largest customer relationships remain sufficiently durable that a material reduction is a downside, not the base case.
2. The Firm prices the transaction on **$11.4m**, not seller-defined EBITDA.
3. Organic revenue can grow without relying on unidentified acquisitions.
4. Margin expands gradually without aggressive add-backs.
5. The **$2.0m** integration program is funded and governed.
6. Billing, WIP and DSO remain manageable through systems consolidation.
7. The capital structure remains solvent under customer or integration downside.
8. The investment works at a **9.0x** exit multiple.

## Priority diligence workstreams

- Complete parent-company customer mapping.
- Validate customer contracts, notice periods and minimum-volume terms.
- Review WIP and AR aging by branch and project.
- Review organic versus acquired growth.
- Review systems, data quality and migration readiness.
- Confirm sustainable EBITDA adjustments.
- Establish normalized working capital.
- Assess management and reporting capability.
- Confirm debt package and covenant definitions.
