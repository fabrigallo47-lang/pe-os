# What failed or was delayed in prior integrations, and what systemic causes remain?

## What resolves it
`observed` post-close project records and management interviews beat the CIM narrative. Patterns:
- ERP migration failures: most common cause of operational degradation post-close
- Data migration issues: customer-master, billing schedule, WIP migration errors
- Team attrition during integration: senior billers and project managers are hardest to replace
Cross-deal standard: any system migration that slipped >6 months = red flag for the next integration.
