# What board approvals and governance rights constrain agent and subsidiary actions post-close?

## What resolves it
`attested` articles of association, shareholder agreement, and subsidiary constitutions.
Key constraints that become PE OS policy-table blockers:
- Reserved matters: acquisitions, capex above threshold, debt incurrence
- Minority rights: any co-investor veto rights survive the acquisition
- Regulatory approval requirements: competition, sector-specific (financial services, defense)
Cross-deal standard: map every reserved matter to a transaction type; wire to the OS policy table.
