# Which balance-sheet liabilities are debt-like vs. ordinary trade payables?

## What resolves it
`attested` legal agreements and `observed` accounting schedules beat headline net-debt figures. Look for:
- Deferred revenue: is it earned or a refundable obligation?
- Earnout liabilities, pension deficits, lease obligations (IFRS 16 vs. economic cash)
- Off-balance-sheet commitments: operating leases, purchase obligations
Cross-deal standard: map every liability >$1m and confirm whether lenders treat it as debt.
