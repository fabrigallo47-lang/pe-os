# What portion of unbilled WIP is collectible vs. aged, disputed, or over-billed?

## What resolves it
`observed` AR aging and invoice dispute logs beat balance-sheet totals. Key tests:
- WIP > 90 days: likely impaired absent a specific explanation
- Disputed invoices: should be excluded from the working-capital peg
- Over-billing patterns: signals delivery risk and future revenue pull-forward
Cross-deal standard: WIP >90 days >15% of total WIP = require reserve analysis.
