# Project Keystone - Diligence Question List

This document records open diligence questions as of the date above. It does not include responses or resolutions.

| Owner area | Question | What it is testing | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial | Which billing accounts share common parent-company relationships? | Tests whether customer concentration is understated by account-level reporting | Open |
| Commercial | What percentage of revenue is generated by the largest parent customer across all billing accounts? | Tests parent-level concentration and downside exposure | Open |
| Commercial | Which customer agreements have minimum-volume commitments, and which can be reduced or terminated on short notice? | Tests durability of revenue and contractual protection | Open |
| Commercial | How much revenue is scheduled/programmatic versus repeat-project versus new/discrete project work? | Tests the quality of the 72% recurring-or-repeat claim | Open |
| Commercial | Which top customers have non-standard pricing, margin or service-level terms? | Tests customer profitability and service risk | Open |
| Financial | Which EBITDA adjustments are implemented, supportable and non-recurring? | Tests sustainability of adjusted EBITDA | Open |
| Financial | What is the support for the pricing and utilization initiative add-back? | Tests whether run-rate operational improvements are historical earnings | Open |
| Financial | What is the correct normalized working-capital target? | Tests cash-free/debt-free economics and purchase-price adjustment | Open |
| Financial | What portion of unbilled WIP is aged, disputed or missing customer approval? | Tests collectability and revenue quality | Open |
| Financial | Which liabilities are debt-like and which are ordinary working-capital items? | Tests no double counting between purchase-price schedules | Open |
| Operational | Which time-entry, billing, ERP and customer-master systems are still active? | Tests integration readiness | Open |
| Operational | What failed or was delayed in prior integrations and systems migrations? | Tests execution risk in the current integration plan | Open |
| Operational | Who owns the post-close integration program and what is the required cash budget? | Tests management capacity and governance | Open |
| Operational | How are utilization, rework and project profitability defined across branches? | Tests comparability of operating KPIs | Open |
| Legal | Which contracts require change-of-control notice or consent? | Tests closing conditions and customer disruption risk | Open |
| Legal | What is the treatment of the founder-related headquarters lease? | Tests related-party economics and lease normalization | Open |
| Debt | What EBITDA basis will lenders use for covenant calculations? | Tests financing headroom and covenant definitions | Open |
| Debt | What are the proposed limits on add-backs, cash netting, acquisition capacity and minimum liquidity? | Tests covenant flexibility and downside protection | Open |
| Governance | Which executives and branch leaders require retention arrangements? | Tests post-close continuity | Open |
| Governance | What board approvals should be required before acquisitions or major systems cutovers? | Tests whether accepted risks will be governed after closing | Open |
