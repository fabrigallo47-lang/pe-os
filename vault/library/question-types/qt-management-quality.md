# Does the management team have the depth and incentives to execute the plan?

## What resolves it
`attested` references from independent directors, prior investors, or auditors beat management self-assessment.
Look for: depth below the founder/CEO (what happens if one person leaves?); incentive alignment (equity, clawback, good/bad leaver); track record of hitting projections vs. reality (compare prior-year budget to actuals over 3 years).
Cross-deal standard: a team passing diligence has at least: a CFO independent of the founder, two functional leaders with >5 years tenure, and MIP that cliff-vests on exit rather than time.
Red flag: management team assembled within 6 months of process launch; key person = also the primary customer relationship.
