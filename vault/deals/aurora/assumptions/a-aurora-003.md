# New bookings land near the FY26 plan of €12.4m

## Why the deal rests on this
The thesis presents new logos as optional ("the plan survives a full stop in new logos"), but the underwritten numbers do not: NRR of 115% supplies only ~15 points of growth, so compounding above 25% ([[a-aurora-002]]) arithmetically requires roughly 10+ points from new bookings. The model's 28% growth derivation ([[c-aurora-002]]) rests directly on management's €12.4m bookings assertion ([[c-aurora-004]]), while the Q2 run-rate implies only €9.8m ([[c-aurora-007]]) — an unresolved contradiction. If bookings land at run-rate, the growth assumption fails even with retention intact. Tested by [[q-aurora-pipeline]].
