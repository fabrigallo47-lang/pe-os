# Is the company's competitive position durable, and is the addressable market real?

## What resolves it
`attested` third-party market data (Gartner, Frost & Sullivan, procurement data) beats management's TAM slides.
Look for: market share defined on a basis that flatters (geography, segment, vintage); competitive moat described as "brand" or "relationships" without pricing power evidence; TAM that includes segments the company has never won.
Cross-deal standard: a durable position requires at least two of: switching cost, certified/qualified-supplier status, contractual exclusivity, or demonstrable pricing premium vs. peers.
Space/defence/industrials: regulatory qualification (e.g. ESA, NATO, AS9100) is a legitimate moat — verify it is not routinely re-opened.
Red flag: market share claim unsupported by named competitors losing business to the company.
