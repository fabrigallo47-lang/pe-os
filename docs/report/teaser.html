Private briefing · July 2026

Every AI tool makes the analysis faster.
None of them makes the firm smarter.

The market is crowded with copilots that read documents and draft memos — work that is becoming free for everyone at the same time. We are building something else: the operating layer where the firm's deals live, reason, and remember.

01The architecture

One brain. Living deals. A loop that closes.

A deal today is scattered across six workstreams, four tools, and nine inboxes — integrated only briefly, imperfectly, in the deal lead's head. And when the deal ends, what the firm learned evaporates. The structure below is designed so that neither ever happens again.

Compounds forever

The Firm Brain

The permanent memory: companies, people, the observed relationship graph, and every lesson the firm has ever paid for — held so that any future deal that raises the same doubt finds it instantly. It never leaves the firm. It is the asset.

One per deal

Living Deal Intelligence

Each deal runs as a living body — its open questions, its evidence, its state of play — always integrated, always current, visible to everyone at once. Understanding no longer decays between meetings; it deepens.

Always watching

Perception & Decision

Documents, models, meetings, and the outside world flow in continuously — no one types anything into a system, ever. Human decisions and their eventual outcomes flow back up into the brain. For the first time, the firm learns as a function.

02What it feels like

Three moments no product on the market can produce.

The answer arrives before the question.

The system doesn't wait to be asked. When something arrives that changes a deal — a filing, a number, a silence — the right person knows, with the reason it matters, at the moment it matters. Pull becomes push.

Three numbers that never met.

The deck says one thing. The model says another. The management call said a third. Nobody ever put them side by side, because nobody holds all three at once. It does — and it tells you which one shows its working.

A ledger of what you chose not to know.

Every deal closes with risks knowingly accepted — and then forgotten. Here they are held. When one resurfaces months later, in a negotiation or in the portfolio, you are told: this is the same risk, and here is what was said about it at the time.

03Under the hood

Engineered as a system, not a chatbot.

Four principles carry the architecture. The rest is implementation.

A knowledge graph, not a document pile.

Every fact in the system is a typed node with provenance — who said it, where, when, and what it bears on. Retrieval follows the structure of the reasoning, not keyword similarity. This is what makes multi-step answers reliable where standard retrieval fails.

Event-driven agents, not a prompt box.

Agents subscribe to what changes — an arriving document, a moved assumption, an external signal — and act when conditions match. Ambient by design: the system operates on standing context, not on questions someone remembered to ask.

Bounded authority, auditable by construction.

What an agent may do alone and what requires a human is written down before anything runs, and every action carries its provenance. Perception is autonomous; speaking for the firm never is. This is what makes agents deployable where an LP is watching.

The firm owns the brain.

The memory layer is the firm's asset — it lives under the firm's control and compounds privately. Models are interchangeable tenants; the structure they flow through is not.

04The mechanism

How evidence, judgment, and outcomes are structured so that the loop compounds — the part that makes this defensible — is the product itself, and it is deliberately not in this document. It is shown, working, on a real deal.
