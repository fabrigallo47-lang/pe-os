# NRR stays at or above 115% through the 5-year hold

## Why the deal rests on this
The thesis is explicitly retention-led: growth is durable *because* it comes from the installed base, and the plan is claimed to survive a full stop in new logos only if NRR ≥ 115% holds. This is the single number the whole structure leans on — if NRR falls materially below 115%, [[q-aurora-growth-real]] resolves refuted by arithmetic alone and the 3x-at-flat-multiple return collapses. The current belief rests solely on management's asserted 12% gross churn ([[c-aurora-005]]); it is directly contradicted by observed evidence ([[c-aurora-003]], [[c-aurora-008]], [[c-aurora-009]]) putting churn near 18% in recent cohorts, which is why [[q-aurora-retention]] is the load-bearing open question.

- v2 (2026-07-13): NRR ≥ 105% (revised: cohort churn evidence) — expert call: churn ~18% vs presented 12%
