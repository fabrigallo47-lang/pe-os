# What is the correct normalized working-capital peg for the business?

## What resolves it
`observed` trailing 12-month actuals beat seasonal spot balances. Framework:
- Calculate monthly average AR, AP, and accrued revenue over the trailing 4-8 quarters
- Adjust for growth (AR days may expand with revenue) and any platform-specific billing cycles
- The peg drives the dollar adjustment at close if actual WC differs — material if business is volatile
Cross-deal standard: use the 12-month average; document any months excluded and why.
