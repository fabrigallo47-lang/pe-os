# Ownership and Boundaries

## Fabri

Fabri owns the path from source files to admitted, executable input:

- native decoding and provenance;
- claim extraction and semantic identity;
- Case Position and support-route formation;
- workbook formula and dependency compilation;
- Current Live Investment Case;
- execution mapping;
- admission manifest;
- proposed/admitted event payloads;
- explicit coverage limits.

Fabri does not produce Candidate state, policy evaluation, human stops or settlement.

## Anto

Anto owns the deterministic state transition:

- applicability after admission;
- support-route evaluation and retraction;
- causal propagation;
- formula execution, rule switches, controls and solver behavior;
- affected set and ordered transitions;
- materiality and authority evaluation;
- Candidate state;
- human stops and blocked components;
- Current/Approved treatment;
- settlement and replay hash.

Anto does not reinterpret raw documents or silently repair compiler semantics.

## Alex

Alex owns the investment-domain constitution and product experience:

- correct meaning of Case Positions, decisions, conditions and accepted risk;
- professional judgment boundaries;
- what a fund needs to see and do;
- role projection and product navigation;
- domain correctness of the demo and acceptance cases;
- practitioner testing and category language.

Alex should not manually patch outputs between components. A repeated correction must become a contract, invariant or regression test.

## Frontend

V17 owns:

- navigation and progressive disclosure;
- rendering backend state;
- collecting explicit review, decision and execution actions;
- showing provenance, coverage and uncertainty;
- local demo fixtures and test tooling.

V17 does not own institutional truth, calculations or authority.
