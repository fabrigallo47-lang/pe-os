# Extractor-to-Screen Contract - V20

## Scope

The compiler/Case Store must produce immutable source versions, exact locators, semantic identity, question bindings, coverage and review objects. V20 extends this boundary to conversation-heavy venture diligence without changing the frontend's fixture-free rule.

## Source and interaction objects

The compiler may produce document, spreadsheet and interaction sources. An interaction contains participants and versioned utterances. Every utterance resolves to:

- interaction ID;
- participant/speaker ID;
- role and affiliation valid at that time;
- start/end timestamp or equivalent locator;
- transcript version;
- attribution confidence;
- `effective_date` and `known_at`;
- confidentiality and processing policy.

The transcript can support the observed fact that a statement occurred. The extracted domain proposition remains `asserted` unless independent evidence establishes a stronger class.

## Claims and semantic identity

Every decision-relevant claim must include its exact source locator, epistemic class, period/perimeter/definition identity where relevant, `bears_on` question bindings, `effective_date`, `known_at`, and review/admission state. Unbound material claims remain visible as pipeline uncertainty.

## Compiler proposal objects

The compiler may emit proposals, never institutional truth:

- Candidate Discrepancy;
- deterministic Derivation;
- AI Hypothesis;
- Spine Change Proposal;
- Agent Mission draft.

A discrepancy must expose aligned and non-aligned semantic dimensions. A derivation must expose inputs, formula/function, unit, assumptions and method hash. An AI hypothesis remains asserted, review-required and non-propagating until admitted.

## Technical validation and conditions

Deep-tech claims may be bounded by Validation Envelopes containing target class, environment, configuration, method and test status. A `CONDITIONS` edge invalidates or gates a proposition when its condition fails; it is not a quantitative `DRIVES` edge.

## Venture finance

The compiler projection may include typed round, cap-table, dilution, runway, milestone, governance and reserve objects. Scenario values remain Working until admitted through the normal state path.

## Boundary law

The frontend accepts only a validated projection. Raw source truth remains immutable; professional corrections and admissions are separate write objects. No domain fact may enter the connected core except through the validated projection boundary.
