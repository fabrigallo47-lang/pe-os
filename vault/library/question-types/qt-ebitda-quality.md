# Which EBITDA adjustments are non-recurring and independently verifiable?

## What resolves it
`attested` QoE report beats management-proposed add-backs. Framework:
- Non-recurring: one-time, externally verifiable, not expected to recur under new ownership
- Suspect patterns: owner compensation normalization, project-phase costs, litigation settlements
- The QoE normalization is the basis for debt capacity — disputes here ripple into leverage and price
Cross-deal standard: any add-back >$1m or >10% of EBITDA requires independent corroboration.
