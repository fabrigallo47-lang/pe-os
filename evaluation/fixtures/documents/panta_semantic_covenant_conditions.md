# Project Harbor covenant terms

Synthetic, non-sensitive evaluation fixture. Monetary amounts are USD millions.

## Financial covenants

At each quarter end, the borrower must maintain a Total Net Leverage Ratio of no more than 5.50x and minimum liquidity of at least $8.0 million.

## Permitted acquisitions

An acquisition is permitted only if the pro forma Total Net Leverage Ratio does not exceed 4.50x immediately after completion.

The 6.00x ratio shown in the drafting example is illustrative only and is not an operative covenant.
