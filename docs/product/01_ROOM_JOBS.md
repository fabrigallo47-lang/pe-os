# PANTA Room Jobs

The rooms share one case but serve different investor mental tasks. Preserve those differences.

## Deal Home
**Question:** Where does the case stand?
Default operating surface. Shows whole case, meaningful changes, unresolved frontier, work, decision context, and proactive Findings.

## Workstream Focus
**Question:** What is happening inside this part of the case?
Shows internal reasoning structure, evolution, selected issue, work, and where depth is needed.

## Trace
**Question:** Why do we believe this?
Deep evidence inspection: source/basis, reading, limitations, and where it matters.

## Simulate
**Question:** What happens to the case if this changes?
Counterfactual sandbox. Same case, changed assumption, truthful bounded propagation. Live case unchanged.

## Review & Admit
**Question:** Should this evidence/Finding change the institutional case?
PANTA proposes; human admits, corrects, or rejects where governance requires it.

## Resolve
**Question:** What evidence-producing work can reduce this uncertainty?
Evidence paths, owners, proposals, what each route can change, and what remains open.

## Formation
**Question:** What case did PANTA build from the raw material?
Case assembly: source material crystallizes into a proposed case with explicit holes and human structural review.

## Replay & Decision
**Replay:** What did we actually know then?
The same case rewinds as-of time.

**Decision:** What judgment are we recording now?
Human decision + rationale + frozen case state. No machine recommendation.

## Outputs
**Question:** How is the live case rendered into the work product I actually use?
IC Memo = live document/authoring surface.
Model = spreadsheet/financial instrument.
Decision Pack = decision artifact.

Outputs are not disconnected files.
