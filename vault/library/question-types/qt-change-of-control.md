# Which contracts require change-of-control notice or consent, and what is the process?

## What resolves it
`attested` contract extracts and legal memo beat management estimates. Critical exposures:
- Government or regulated-sector contracts often require novation or agency consent
- Material customer contracts: >5% of revenue, need to know if they can terminate on change
- Key supplier agreements: platform dependencies that could be cut off
Cross-deal standard: any contract >$2m or >3% of revenue should be reviewed for CoC provisions.
