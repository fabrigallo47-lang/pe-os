# Is the business exposed to regulatory change or licensing risk that could impair the plan?

## What resolves it
`attested` legal opinion, regulatory filing history, or industry body confirmation beats management assertion that "we are compliant."
Look for: licensing that is issuer-discretionary (can be revoked without cause); export control exposure (ITAR, EAR, dual-use) for defence/space companies; environmental or safety permits that are site-specific and non-transferable on change-of-control; government contract terms that require re-bidding on ownership change.
Cross-deal standard: regulatory risk is material if any single licence or approval accounts for >20% of revenue and has a renewal date within the investment horizon.
Space/defence specifics: check ITAR registration status; ESA/NATO security clearance portability on change of ownership; export licence per-contract vs. blanket.
Red flag: "change of control" notification requirement in a government contract without written consent already obtained or waivable under the relevant authority.
