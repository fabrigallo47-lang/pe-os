## Bearing

Every turn of contraction comes directly out of the MOIC — at 25% ARR CAGR, exiting near 4.3x turns the 3x into a 2x and the deal fails the fund's floor even if [[q-aurora-growth-real]] and [[q-aurora-retention]] both resolve supported. If exit-year growth or retention quality is materially below entry-year (which [[q-aurora-growth-real]] bears on), a flat multiple is not the base case.
