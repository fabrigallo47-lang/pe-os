# Provisional Fund Lens Schema v0.1

This file defines **where institution-specific policy may live**. It does not contain or imply any institution's policy.

A Fund Lens can configure:
- mandate and constraints;
- house definitions;
- materiality and evidence standards;
- automatic-admission policy;
- operative-value selection policy;
- economic hurdles and scenario conventions;
- authority and workflow policy;
- toolkit cost/duration and artifact conventions.

It cannot change:
- source facts or provenance;
- append-only history;
- Candidate / Current / Approved separation;
- the meaning of StatedPosition, CaseReading or Decision;
- the frozen Dynamic semantics;
- the rule that decisions and material waivers are human authority events.

The schema is deliberately provisional. It becomes a real Lens only after its values are sourced, reviewed, versioned and approved.
