# Which time-entry, billing, ERP, and customer-master systems does the business rely on?

## What resolves it
`observed` IT walkthrough and `attested` vendor contracts beat org-chart descriptions.
Key failure modes:
- Business relies on systems owned by the seller's parent (TSA dependency post-close)
- Customer master data is fragmented across billing and CRM systems (data quality risk)
- ERP is not enterprise-grade for the size of the business (scalability ceiling)
Cross-deal standard: map system ownership vs. deal perimeter; identify TSA scope and duration.
