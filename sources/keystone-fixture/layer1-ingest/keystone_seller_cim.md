# Project Keystone - Alderstone Compliance & Technical Services

## Executive summary

Alderstone Compliance & Technical Services is a scaled provider of outsourced environmental, health and safety, industrial hygiene, field inspection and technical-compliance services to customers in regulated, safety-critical environments. The Company operates across the Midwest and Southeast United States and has completed four acquisitions since 2020.

Alderstone supports industrial, infrastructure, municipal, healthcare and regulated commercial customers with recurring and repeat technical services. Management expects FY2025E revenue of **$74.0m** and seller-adjusted EBITDA of **$12.7m**, representing a **17.2%** margin.

## Investment highlights

- Scaled regional platform in a fragmented outsourced technical-services market.
- Long-standing customer relationships and high repeat activity in compliance-critical service lines.
- Attractive historical growth supported by pricing, customer expansion and acquisitions.
- Asset-light operating model with capital expenditure of approximately 1%-2% of revenue.
- Identifiable value creation through pricing, utilization, reporting and systems consolidation.
- Meaningful opportunity to consolidate founder-owned regional service providers.
- Founder and senior management expected to retain a meaningful equity interest.

## Value-creation priorities

- Expand scheduled compliance programs and cross-sell service lines across multi-site customers.
- Improve price realization and project scope control.
- Increase utilization through weekly staffing and time-entry discipline.
- Consolidate ERP, project accounting, customer master and billing systems.
- Build national-account coordination.
- Acquire complementary industrial-hygiene, inspection and compliance providers.

## Risk factors disclosed by management

- Customer relationships are generally governed by master agreements and site-level statements of work without guaranteed minimum volumes.
- Acquired branches continue to operate on partially different systems and processes.
- The Company depends on qualified technical personnel and local customer relationships.
- Integration and systems initiatives may require additional time and expenditure.
- Project and customer activity may be affected by customer budgets and facility programs.
