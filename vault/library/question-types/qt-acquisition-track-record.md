# Has management demonstrated the ability to acquire and integrate without destroying value?

## What resolves it
`attested` post-acquisition P&L vs. acquisition model beats management narrative of "smooth integration."
Look for: time from acquisition to full systems integration (ERP, billing, HR); revenue retention post-acquisition (customer attrition in acquired entity first 18 months); whether prior acquisitions are reflected at acquisition cost or have been goodwill-impaired; cultural integration in people-heavy businesses (retention of key staff from acquired entities).
Cross-deal standard: a credible M&A track record requires at least two completed acquisitions with >18 months of post-close data available, showing EBITDA within 20% of the acquisition model.
Roll-up risk: if the business plan calls for >1 acquisition per year, the management team must have run a dedicated integration function, not ad-hoc project teams.
Red flag: prior acquisition followed by cost-only integration (headcount cuts, no revenue synergy delivered); management defines "integration success" as completion of a systems migration, not EBITDA performance.
