# Retention is institutional, not founder-dependent

## Why the deal rests on this
The NRR assumption ([[a-aurora-001]]) only holds through a 5-year hold if retention is a property of the product, not of one person. The CIM attributes low churn to Aurora's "mission-critical position in customer workflows" ([[c-aurora-005]]) — retention as institutional. Observed evidence points the other way: a top-20 reference customer tied their own renewal to the founder personally ([[c-aurora-003]]) and said they would immediately reconsider the contract if he left ([[c-aurora-010]]). If key-account retention is founder-contingent, then any founder transition during the hold breaks [[a-aurora-001]] and, through it, the thesis. Tested by [[q-aurora-mgmt]].
