# Does the lender package give us adequate flexibility to operate and exit within the investment horizon?

## What resolves it
`attested` final credit agreement beats term sheet; independent covenant model beats sponsor model.
Look for: covenant headroom that is measured at the tightest point in the plan (month 9-18 post-close, not year-end); restricted payment basket that limits dividend recaps or management fee extraction; portability clause absent or conditional (blocks clean exit at target multiple); SOFR vs. fixed rate and breakage cost on refinancing.
Cross-deal standard: minimum 25% headroom vs. financial covenant at the tightest projected month; leverage covenant tested quarterly, not annually; change-of-control put at par unless portability language is clean.
Amendment track: if a prior amendment was required, the reason and the lender relationship quality are at least as important as the new covenant level — a lender that extracted equity in an amendment is a trapped partner.
Red flag: covenant EBITDA definition that materially diverges from reported EBITDA (addback creep) — the synthetic headroom disappears in a downturn when add-backs are challenged.
