# PANTA Room Jobs

The rooms share one case but serve different investor mental tasks. Preserve those differences.

## Deal Home
**Question:** Where does the case stand?
Default operating surface. Shows whole case, meaningful changes, unresolved frontier, work, decision context, and proactive Findings.

## Workstream Focus
**Question:** What is happening inside this part of the case?
Shows internal reasoning structure, evolution, selected issue, work, and where depth is needed.

## Trace
**Question:** Why do we believe this?
Deep evidence inspection: source/basis, reading, limitations, and where it matters.

## Simulate
**Question:** What happens to the case if this changes?
Counterfactual sandbox over the investment-case graph. Introduce a hypothetical valid graph change, qualitative or quantitative, and observe the same bounded propagation as the real state-transition engine: changed objects, held objects, contradictions, invalidated support, blocked paths and required human review. Numerical sensitivity is one subset of this room. Live case unchanged.

## Review changes
**Question:** Should this material or judgment-bearing proposal change the institutional case?
Evidence ingestion and clear factual updates are automatic. PANTA groups the exceptional changes that materially reshape the case; an accountable human updates, edits, or dismisses each proposal where governance requires it.

## Resolve
**Question:** What evidence-producing work can reduce this uncertainty?
Evidence paths, owners, proposals, what each route can change, and what remains open.

## Formation
**Question:** What case did PANTA build from the raw material?
Case assembly: source material crystallizes into a proposed case with explicit holes and human structural review.

## Replay & Decision
**Replay:** What did we know and believe at this meaningful point in the case?
Meaningful ledger events rewind the same case in place through `loadCase(caseId, { asOf })`. Workstream position remains stable while readings, questions, gaps, and decision state reflect the selected cutoff.

**Decision:** What still needs judgment before we can make the institutional decision?
The backend-selected decision-critical Questions expose their current reading, unresolved Unknowns, decision relevance, qualitative load-bearingness, severity, fragility, criticality, and any Condition. Only after reviewing them does the authorized human record decision + rationale + conditions against a frozen case state. No machine recommendation or composite score.

## Case changes
**Question:** What changed between two recorded states, when did we know it, and who acted?
The Case Journal joins the institutional timeline with a deterministic comparison of immutable Current states. Effective, knowledge, and recording time remain separate. A closing state is always explicit before later movement can be described as post-close drift.

## Outputs
**Question:** How is the live case rendered into the work product I actually use?
IC Memo = live document/authoring surface.
Model = spreadsheet/financial instrument.
Decision Pack = decision artifact.

Outputs are not disconnected files.
